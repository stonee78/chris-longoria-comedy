Real stories, told loud

Chris Longoria is a stand-up comedian from Corpus Christi, Texas. He has taken the Kill Tony stage twice in 2026, on episodes #770 and #777, and performs on stages from Corpus Christi to Austin. He hosts the That Dude Talks podcast, where guests have included wrestlers Sammy Guevara and Tay Melo, and he brings live comedy to Kiko's every month with Kiko's Comedy Nights.
